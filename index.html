I'm a Ph.D. student in Agricultural, Environmental, and Development Economics at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Alexis H. Villacis 🎓. My research sits at the intersection of causal inference, machine learning, and econometrics, with a recurring focus on quantifying risk. 🛡️.

Before OSU, I earned an M.S. in Statistics and a B.S. in Statistics & Financial Engineering at Korea University, where I worked on portfolio and operational risk. 📈.

Looking ahead, I hope to bring this training in causal inference and quantitative risk modeling to the finance and insurance industry. 💼.

My recent work looks at how AI is reshaping American farming (Choices, 2026, open access) and how climate anomaly can be used to estimate yield distribution.
